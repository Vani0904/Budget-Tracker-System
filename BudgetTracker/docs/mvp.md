# Minimum Viable Product

## Problem
Users need a simple way to record income and expenses and understand their monthly spending without connecting their bank account.

## Target user
An individual managing their own personal finances. With the goal of saving on spending.

## Product goal
Help the user see what they earned, what they spend, and how their spending compares with their chosen monthly limits.

## Included in the first usable web version
- Register, sign in, and sign out.
- Add, view, edit, and delete income and expense transactions.
- Assign transactions to preset categories.
- Filter transactions by month and category.
- Set monthly category spending limits.
- View income, expenses, net income, and budget remaining.
- Use the interface on desktop and mobile browsers.

## Excluded
- Bank synchronisation.
- Automatic categorisation.
- Multiple currencies.
- Shared household budgets.
- Forecasting and financial advice.
- Native mobile applications.
- Offline editing and synchronisation.

## Initial constraints
- C#/.NET-first implementation.
- Local development and testing.
- GBP only.
- Manual transaction entry.
- Use fictional financial data while developing.

## First development milestone
Record income and expenses and calculate correct totals.

This milestone may use a console interface before the web UI exists.
